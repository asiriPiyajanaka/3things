# Code of Conduct

3Things welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Give credit and respect privacy.
- Accept correction gracefully and help keep discussions useful.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

For a private conduct concern, use a private contact channel published by [the maintainer](https://github.com/asiriPiyajanaka). If none is available, open a brief issue requesting private contact without including sensitive details. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This code of conduct applies in the repository and in public spaces where someone is representing the project.
